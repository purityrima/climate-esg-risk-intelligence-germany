# AI-Powered Climate & ESG Risk Intelligence Platform for Multi-Sector Decision Support in Germany

Python | PyTorch | Flask | SHAP | GeoPandas | Explainable AI | ESG | Germany | Status
> AI Engineering project integrating Climate Intelligence, Geospatial Data Science, Explainable Artificial Intelligence, and Deep Learning to generate Regional Climate Risk Scores (RCRS), the National Climate Risk Registry for Germany (NCRR-G), and the Germany Climate Risk Map for climate risk decision support.

## Project Overview

Regional Climate Risk Score (RCRS)
Feed-Forward Neural Network
SHAP Explainability
National Climate Risk Registry for Germany (NCRR-G)
Germany Climate Risk Map

This repository contains the complete source code, datasets, documentation, and experiments for developing an AI-powered Climate Risk Intelligence Platform for Germany. The platform integrates climate hazard and socioeconomic indicators to estimate Regional Climate Risk Scores (RCRS) at the NUTS-3 district level using an explainable Feed-Forward Neural Network (FFNN).

The project harmonizes climate and socioeconomic datasets into a unified modelling framework, constructs an IPCC-inspired Regional Climate Risk Score (RCRS), and applies explainable artificial intelligence (SHAP) to interpret model predictions. The resulting predictions are operationalized through the National Climate Risk Registry for Germany (NCRR-G) and an accompanying Germany Climate Risk Map.

The platform combines PyTorch, GeoPandas, SHAP explainability, and geospatial analytics to support climate risk assessment and decision-making for banking, insurance, government agencies, climate adaptation planning, and research. All trained models, preprocessing pipelines, explainability artefacts, and registry outputs are exported as reproducible deployment-ready assets.
